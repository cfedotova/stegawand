what it is

stegawand is a neural steganography project trained on an Apple Silicon M2 laptop — no servers, no paid APIs. It takes any RGB image and embeds an almost-invisible spatial pattern (residual) that carries a binary message. The decoder reads the message back even after the image has been hit by typical transmission distortions: messenger compression, resize, grayscale conversion, photographing the screen with a camera.

⊹

CDMA spread spectrum

every bit is spread across the entire image with a unique random ±1 code. no pixel “owns” a single bit — full spatial distribution, no cells, no blocks.

✿

content-aware residual

brightness and texture masks hide the perturbation in regions where the human eye is least sensitive. amplitude capped at 5 % of pixel range.

⌘

distortion-resistant

trained with 4 differentiable distortions: gaussian noise, blur 5 × 5, grayscale, fake-JPEG. survives Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram and screenshots.

♡

light on M2

~92 K trainable parameters. full training (12 epochs) takes ~10 min on MPS. inference under 50 ms per 256 × 256 image.

how to use

  1. encode — pick any image, type a secret up to {{ max_chars }} ASCII chars, get back the stego image plus decoding through each distortion separately.
  2. decode — upload a stego image (e.g. one you got back from a Telegram chat) and recover the hidden message.
  3. stress test — slide an intensity knob to find the threshold where the decoder breaks. great for understanding the model’s robustness limits.
  4. theory — formulas, pipeline diagram, visualization of the random ±1 codes, matched-filter decoder explained.
